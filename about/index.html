Every rule links its sources. The rules are written in our own words; the sources are:

  • The documentation of each framework the skills cover

About this site

Plain HTML built by a small Node.js script from the skills and rules in the repository, one stylesheet and two small scripts (this page's copy and search helpers, and the accessibility panel). No cookies, no analytics, no requests to other sites. The panel stores your reader settings in this browser's local storage, on your device only.

Accessibility of this site

The site is built to WCAG 2.2 AA as a target: it works with a keyboard and a screen reader, follows your light or dark preference and reduced motion, and reflows down to 320 pixels. If something stands in your way, please open an issue.

Built from version 0.1.0 of the repository.
